Measurement boundaries and limits

Latency uses nearest-rank percentiles (ceil(p × n) − 1), with no interpolation. “Early” and “late” are the first and last 10% of a complete batch. Reciprocal throughput is not tail latency. Sequential replay excludes network and queueing delay and cannot establish overload capacity.

The million-event feasibility trial is streaming cancellation churn, seed 42. It includes parsing and is not an engine-only comparison. Other million-event workloads were not attempted.

Single machine, sequential run order, no confidence intervals. Synthetic teaching submissions are not calibrated exchange records. No fees, balances, self-trade checks or concurrency.
